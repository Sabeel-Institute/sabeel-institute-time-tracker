Sabeel Time Tracker

Sabeel Time Tracker is where staff and volunteers of Sabeel Institute record the hours they work for the institute and submit them for approval each week. It is a private application for the institute's own team, not a public service.

Getting an account

Sign in on this website with your @oursabeel.com Google account. An administrator then approves you; until they do, you will see a “waiting for approval” screen, which lets you in by itself the moment they approve.

“This account isn't set up for the app yet”

The phone app signs in existing accounts only. Sign in on this website once first, as above, and then the app will work.

Getting the app

Android: the download page, or Google Play by invitation from your administrator. iPhone: TestFlight, by invitation from your administrator.

An Android phone with the app from the download page older than version 0.28.0 needs it uninstalled once before a newer one installs, from either place. Nothing is lost: everything is kept online.

Reporting a problem

To report something or someone in the app, or anything else that is wrong, use Report a problem on the app's Home screen. It goes to the institute's administrators, who reply by email within 24 hours.

Contact

Email privacy@oursabeel.com for anything about your account, your information, or deleting your account.
