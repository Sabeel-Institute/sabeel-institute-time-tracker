Privacy Policy

Sabeel Time Tracker · last updated 15 October 2026

Sabeel Time Tracker is a private application in which staff and volunteers of Sabeel Institute record the hours they work for the institute. It is not a public service. Only accounts the institute has approved can use it.

What we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Your name, your email address, and the link to your Google profile picture Your Google account, when you sign in To identify you to the colleagues who approve your hours
The hours you record: when you started and stopped, the activity, an optional note, and the time zone you worked in You It is the content of the application
Your weekly timesheets: when you submitted them, and who approved or returned them, with any reason given You, and the colleague who reviews your hours The institute's record of approved hours
A notification address for each device you turn notifications on for, and which notifications you want Your device, and your settings To tell you about your timesheets on that device
Crash and error diagnostics The app, automatically So faults can be found and fixed

Crash reports carry your internal account identifier and nothing else — no name, no email address, no hours.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for their own purposes.

Who else can see it

These service providers process this information on the institute's behalf, under agreements requiring them to protect it to the same standard set out here and to use it only to provide their service:

  • Google — sign-in.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase — where the application and its data are hosted, in the United States, and the delivery of notifications to Android phones and web browsers.
  • Apple — the delivery of notifications to iPhones.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics.

How long we keep it

The hours recorded in the application, the timesheets and the record of who approved them belong to Sabeel Institute rather than to the person who entered them, and are retained as organisational records. That remains true after somebody leaves.

Your account is a different thing: your sign-in, your profile, your contact details, your device registrations and your notification settings are removed when your account is removed.

Deleting your account

Email privacy@oursabeel.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and act within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your ability to sign in, your profile, your contact details, your device registrations and your notification settings. It does not remove the organisational records described above, which the institute retains.

You can also ask us at the same address what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.

Children

This application is not intended for and is not offered to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and tell account holders by email.
